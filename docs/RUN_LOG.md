### 📚 Reference Paper Horizons & Datasets (2026-02-20)

**TAFAS (Kim et al., AAAI 2025):**
- Datasets: ETTh1, ETTm1, ETTh2, ETTm2, Exchange, Illness, Weather (7 total)
- Horizons: H ∈ {96, 192, 336, 720} (non-Illness), H ∈ {24, 36, 48, 60} (Illness)
- Lookback: L = 96 (L = 36 for Illness)
- Backbones: iTransformer, PatchTST, DLinear, OLS, FreTS, MICN (6 architectures)
- Splits: 60:20:20 for ETT, 70:10:20 for Exchange/Illness/Weather
- Seeds: 3 pre-training seeds, best selected by validation MSE

**DynaTTA (Grover & Etemad, ICML 2025):**
- Datasets: ETTh1, ETTh2, ETTm1, Weather, Exchange (5 total, same as TAFAS minus ETTm2 and Illness)
- Horizons: H ∈ {96, 192, 336, 720} — same as TAFAS
- Lookback: L = 96
- Backbones: same 5 as TAFAS (iTransformer, PatchTST, DLinear, OLS, FreTS)
- Also benchmarked on TTFBench (perturbed versions of same datasets)
- Ablations done on ETTh1, ETTh2, ETTm1 at H ∈ {96, 720}

**Implication for RGTTA:**
- Our horizons {96, 192, 336} overlap with 3 of their 4. We are **missing H=720**.
- Our datasets overlap: we have ETTh1/h2/m1/m2 + Weather + Exchange = 6 of their 7 (missing Illness).
- Both papers use standard ETT/Weather/Exchange — same as us ✅
- Our 8 synthetic datasets are unique to us (no overlap with TAFAS/DynaTTA).
- **Consider adding H=720** in a future run for direct comparability.
